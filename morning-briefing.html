Morning Briefing

Five golden lines, delivered the frogman way. Eight minutes, then go win the day.

Box breathe

Ready?tap start

In through the nose for 4. Hold for 4. Out for 4. Hold for 4. Follow the red dot around the box. Do 4 rounds (about a minute) to calm the body before you speak.

The Big Four · your morning route

1Goal

Pick ONE small win for today. Make it tiny and clear: "Workout done by 7."

2See it

60 seconds, eyes closed. Watch yourself do it, including the moment you want to quit, and push through it.

3Say it

Speak the five golden lines below, out loud, 3 times each.

4Control

Finish with one more round of box breathing. Calm body, sharp mind. Go.

The five golden lines

    1st timeSay it as "I." Steady and clear.
    2nd timeSay it to yourself by name, like a coach.
    3rd timeFull intent. Squeeze your fist on the last word.

    Tap a card each time you say it. Stand tall, speak low and slow, and pause after each one so it lands.

    The Big Four (goal setting, mental rehearsal, self-talk, arousal control) is the mental-toughness framework reported from Navy SEAL training. Box breathing is the breath-control drill widely taught by former SEALs. The lines are built to research rules: believable, built on proof, and naming the obstacle.
